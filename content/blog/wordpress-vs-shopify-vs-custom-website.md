Short answer: choose **WordPress** for most business websites with a blog or many service pages, **Shopify** if selling products online is the main job and you want the easiest store to run, and a **custom build** when you need something the platforms do badly, like a booking engine, a portal or very high speed at scale.

## Side by side {#compare}

|  | WordPress | Shopify | Custom (e.g. Next.js) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Best for | Business sites, blogs, WooCommerce stores | Online stores, D2C brands | Unique features, portals, high traffic |
| Upfront cost | Low – medium | Low – medium | Highest |
| Monthly platform fee | None (you pay hosting) | Yes, plus paid apps | None (you pay hosting) |

| You own | Everything | Content; platform is rented | Everything |
Table: General comparison for small and mid-sized businesses in India. Check current Shopify plan prices before you decide.

## When WordPress is the right choice {#wordpress}

WordPress runs a large share of the web for a reason: it is flexible, has a plugin for almost everything, and your team can edit pages without a developer. It suits clinics, schools, CA and law firms, manufacturers and service businesses that need many pages and a blog. The cost is upkeep: plugins and the core need regular updates, and a site overloaded with plugins gets slow.

With WooCommerce, WordPress also becomes a capable store with no platform fee. See our [WordPress and WooCommerce](/wordpress-development) packages.

## When Shopify is the right choice {#shopify}

Shopify is the fastest way to a reliable online store. Hosting, security and checkout are handled for you, and day-to-day running is simple. You pay a monthly subscription and often for apps, and you are limited to what themes and apps allow. It suits fashion, beauty, food and D2C brands where the store is the business.

Compare store costs in our [e-commerce website cost guide](/blog/ecommerce-website-cost-india).

## When a custom build is worth it {#custom}

A custom site (we build these with React and Next.js) costs more upfront but has no plugin bloat and no platform limits. It is worth it when you need custom logic: a booking or quote engine, a client portal, B2B pricing, complex integrations, or top speed at high traffic. For a standard 5–10 page business site, it is usually more than you need.

## Decide in five minutes {#decide}

1. **Is selling products online the main job?** Yes, and you want the simplest setup: Shopify. Yes, and you also want lots of content or no monthly fee: WooCommerce.
2. **Do you need features no plugin does well?** Bookings with custom rules, portals, logins, calculators: custom.
3. **Otherwise** WordPress, built carefully so it stays fast.
