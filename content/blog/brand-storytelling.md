## The death of traditional ads {#death-of-traditional-ads}

Gen Z can spot inauthentic marketing instantly. Traditional, interruption-based advertising no longer works. To connect with this demographic, brands must pivot to authentic storytelling that aligns with values and culture.

## Authenticity is key {#authenticity-is-key}

It's not about selling a product; it's about inviting the customer into a narrative. User Generated Content (UGC) and behind-the-scenes content perform exceptionally well because they feel unpolished and real.
