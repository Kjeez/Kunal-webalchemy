## The art of conversion {#the-art-of-conversion}

Traffic is vanity; conversion is sanity. Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) is the systematic process of increasing the percentage of website visitors who take a desired action. It's not about guessing—it's about data-driven decisions.

## 5 quick wins for CRO {#quick-wins}

Implementing these changes can yield immediate results:

- **Clear Value Proposition:** State clearly what you do and typically "above the fold."
- **Reduced Form Fields:** Remove unnecessary fields. Each field removed can increase conversions by 10%.
- **Social Proof:** Add testimonials, logos, and case studies to build trust.
