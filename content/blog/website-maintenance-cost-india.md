Short answer: keeping a small business website online in India costs about **₹3,000 to ₹8,000 a year** for the domain and hosting. If you add a maintenance plan (updates, backups, security and small edits), expect roughly **₹12,000 to ₹40,000 a year** in total. E-commerce stores and busy sites cost more.

## What website maintenance should include {#includes}

- **Updates.** WordPress core, theme and plugin updates, tested so nothing breaks.
- **Backups.** Automatic daily or weekly backups stored off the server, with a tested restore.
- **Security.** Malware scanning, firewall, spam protection on forms, and quick clean-up if something goes wrong.
- **Uptime and speed checks.** Alerts if the site goes down, and periodic speed tuning.
- **Small content changes.** New photos, prices, team members, offers or a new page each month.
- **Form and lead checks.** Making sure contact forms and WhatsApp buttons still deliver enquiries. A broken form costs more than any maintenance plan.

## Do you need an AMC (annual maintenance contract)? {#need-amc}

You probably need one if your site runs on WordPress with several plugins, takes payments, or you update it every month and don’t want to do it yourself. You can probably skip it if your site is small, rarely changes, and your developer offers pay-per-change edits.

## Signs your website isn’t being maintained {#signs}

- The footer still shows a copyright year from years ago.
- Your browser shows a “Not secure” warning.
- Pages load slowly on mobile, or images are broken.
- You stopped getting form enquiries and nobody noticed.
- You don’t know who has the hosting and domain logins.

## Five ways to cut your yearly website costs {#save}

1. Register the domain for several years at once, in your own name.
2. Use free SSL instead of paying for a certificate.
3. Remove plugins you don’t use; each one is a cost and a security risk.
4. Pick hosting sized for your traffic, not the most expensive plan.
5. Batch small edits into one monthly request instead of paying per change.
