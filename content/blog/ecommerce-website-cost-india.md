Short answer: a small online store in India (up to about 50 products) usually costs **₹25,000 to ₹60,000** to build. Larger catalogues and custom features run from ₹60,000 to ₹2 lakh and more. On top of that come running costs: hosting or a platform subscription, payment gateway fees on every order, and shipping.

This guide covers what the build costs, how Shopify, WooCommerce and custom stores compare, and the monthly costs that decide whether your store is actually profitable.

For most small and mid-sized Indian businesses we recommend WooCommerce or Shopify. Custom stores make sense when you need something the platforms can’t do well, such as B2B pricing tiers, complex product configurators or deep ERP integration.

## Running costs most people forget {#running}

- **Payment gateway fees.** Razorpay, PayU, Cashfree and CCAvenue typically charge around 2% per transaction on standard domestic plans, more for international cards. Check each provider’s current rates and settlement times.
- **Shipping and COD.** Shipping aggregators charge per shipment, plus a COD fee. Returns (RTO) on COD orders can quietly eat your margin.
- **Hosting.** A WooCommerce store needs better hosting than a brochure site, typically ₹5,000 to ₹15,000 a year for a small store.
- **Apps and plugins.** Reviews, WhatsApp notifications, invoicing and abandoned-cart tools are often paid monthly.
- **Product photography and data entry.** Good photos sell. Budget for them, or for someone to upload and describe your catalogue.
- **Marketing.** A store with no traffic makes no sales. Plan for SEO, social media or ads from day one.

## What pushes the build cost up {#drivers}

- Number of products and variants (sizes, colours, materials).
- COD, multiple payment gateways and EMI options.
- Shipping integration with automatic rates and tracking.
- GST-compliant invoices and B2B (wholesale) pricing.
- Inventory sync with a physical store, POS or ERP.
- Subscriptions, gift cards, loyalty points or custom product builders.

## Before you build: a quick checklist {#checklist}

1. GST registration and a current account for gateway settlements.
2. Return, refund, shipping and privacy policies (payment gateways ask for these).
3. Product photos, names, prices and descriptions in a spreadsheet.
4. A decision on COD and which pin codes you ship to.
5. A plan for your first 100 orders: who you will market to, and how.
