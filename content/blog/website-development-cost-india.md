Short answer: a professional small-business website in India usually costs **₹15,000 to ₹50,000** as a one-time build, plus roughly ₹3,000 to ₹8,000 a year for the domain and hosting. A basic template site from a freelancer can cost under ₹10,000. Custom-built sites with bookings, logins or integrations start around ₹1 lakh.

The range is wide because "a website" can mean a one-page profile or a full sales system. Below is what each type typically costs, what pushes the price up, the yearly costs nobody quotes upfront, and exactly what we charge.

## What actually drives the price {#drivers}

- **Number of unique page designs.** Ten pages that share one layout cost far less than ten pages that each need their own design.
- **Template or custom design.** A customised theme is cheaper and faster. A design made from scratch for your brand takes longer and costs more, but looks like no one else.
- **Who writes the content.** If you supply text and photos, the price stays low. Professional copywriting typically adds ₹15,000 to ₹40,000 for a full site.
- **Features.** Online booking, payments, multiple languages, CRM or WhatsApp API integrations, member logins and calculators all add development time.
- **Platform.** WordPress and Shopify are quicker to build on. Custom code (React, Next.js) costs more upfront but gives you full control and speed.
- **Who builds it.** Freelancers are cheapest, large agencies the most expensive. Small studios sit in between with a process and support.
- **SEO and speed work.** A site built to rank (structure, schema, fast loading, local SEO setup) takes more effort than one that only looks good.

## The yearly costs most quotes leave out {#yearly}

The build is a one-time cost, but every website has running costs. Before you sign, ask: "What will I pay in year two?"

| Item | Typical yearly cost | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Domain (.in or .com) | ₹600 – ₹1,500 | First-year offers are often cheap; check the renewal price. |
| Hosting | ₹2,000 – ₹8,000 | Shared hosting is fine for most small sites; stores need more. |
| SSL certificate | Free – ₹5,000 | Free SSL is perfectly fine for most businesses. |
| Business email | ₹0 – ₹2,000 per user | Google Workspace or Zoho; some hosts include basic mail. |
| Maintenance & updates | ₹0 – ₹36,000 | Updates, backups and small content changes. |
| Paid plugins / themes | ₹0 – ₹10,000 | Renewals for premium WordPress plugins, if used. |

## Freelancer, agency or DIY website builder? {#who-builds}

- **DIY builders (Wix, Shopify, Squarespace, etc.).** Low upfront cost, but monthly subscriptions add up over the years and the time cost is yours. Good if you enjoy doing it yourself.
- **Freelancer.** Usually the cheapest custom option. Check live sites they have built, and make sure the domain and hosting are registered in your name in case they become unavailable later.
- **Studio or agency.** Costs more than a freelancer, but you get a team, a process, a written timeline and someone accountable for support after launch.

## How to avoid overpaying (or underpaying) {#avoid}

1. **Own your domain.** It should be registered in your name, with the login shared with you.
2. **Get inclusions in writing.** Number of pages, rounds of revisions, who writes content, SEO setup, and months of free support.
3. **Ask for year-two costs.** A ₹4,999 website with a ₹9,999 yearly renewal is not cheap.
4. **Open their past work on your phone.** Most of your visitors will be on mobile. If their sites are slow there, yours will be too.
5. **Insist on admin access.** You should be able to log in, edit text and add pages without depending on the developer.
6. **Pay in stages.** A deposit to start and the balance at launch is standard. We take 50% upfront and 50% when the site goes live.
